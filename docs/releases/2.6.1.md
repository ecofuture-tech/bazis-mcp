# bazis-mcp 2.6.1

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Changes

- **The catalog is bazis-front 0.3.1** (it was 0.3.0): `package_guide`, `guide_search` and
  `front_catalog` describe its fixes of the components, of the generated screens and of the
  visual check, found by the live runs of 0.3.0 (its notes, `docs/releases/0.3.1.md`).
  The other packages of the catalog are unchanged, bazis-cli is not in it.
  - The related lists show their values as the list of their entity: `RelatedList` takes
    `display` and `icon`, a sum has the format and the currency of its field.
  - The visual check has the codes V017 (a text of a board or a column cut by an ellipsis,
    a warning), V018 (a table that scrolls sideways once its texts are long, a warning) and
    V019 (the page loaded again between its check and its screenshot, an error). A screen
    is shot again once in those cases, and `report.json` says so with `retried`.
  - The dev server of the end-to-end tests optimizes its dependencies as it starts, with a
    cache of its own (`node_modules/.vite-e2e`).
- **`e2e_data` only adds**, the rule of the guide of a product: it never deletes a user or
  a record, it finds its records by a key of its own and creates those that are missing.
  The scenarios pass again on the same database: a generated test finds the items it
  created by their ids, and `openItem` shows more items of a column until it has its item.
- The new warnings of the scenarios of the end-to-end tests: P033 (a scenario changes an
  item it did not create) and P034 (a scenario finds an item it did not create by its texts).
