# bazis-mcp 2.10.1

## Fixes

- The rule of the users app in `bazis://rules` says what the app keeps: the user app of the
  project is named `users` and keeps `users/routes.py` and `users/router.py` (and a user
  model of its own in `users/models.py`), and the root router module registers
  `router.register('users.router')`, which also declares the token endpoint (`users.E002`).
  The wording is clearer; the rule is the same.
- A product with a frontend made by `bazis new` (bazis-cli 2.6.1) gets the app `users/` of
  the team as well: `write_files` writes it, as `create_project` does.

## Changes

- The other packages and their versions are unchanged; the catalog is unchanged.
