# bazis-mcp 2.7.0

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **The public screens** of bazis-front 0.4.0, the screens of the users who do not log in.
  The rules of the frontend (`bazis://rules/frontend`) say how an agent builds them:
  - The guests are an anonymous role of `spec/product.yaml` (`anonymous: true`, without a
    `test_user`), whose permit role is `Role(for_anonymous=True)` with permissions of the
    selector `all` only, or none when the guests use only the public actions (no `access`
    then): a role for anonymous users that views the entities is never created when the
    guests do not read them, it would open their data to anyone (P030).
  - A screen is public when its `roles` lists that role, and only then: `bazis_front
    screens` routes it outside the session (`PUBLIC_ROUTES`), never a route or a layout of
    the product in `frontend/src/app/router.tsx`. The checks it must pass: S047 (an
    anonymous role that no screen lists), P035 (a scenario of the role that opens a screen
    that is not public) and S045 (a public screen that leads to one that is not).
  - A guest only reads through the routes of the entities (bazis-permit refuses him the
    creates, the changes and the deletions, S044) and writes only through a custom action
    of the route set declared `public=True` (S046), limited by a throttle
    (`BAZIS_THROTTLE_PUBLIC` or its own); a transit of a guest is a route set of its own
    with `action_transit` declared again `public=True` (bazis-statusy). `permit.W007` and
    `permit.W008` of `run_doctor` are fixed, the common channel of bazis-ws is open to
    anonymous sessions only when it is public, and a guest who needs an account is given
    one (no package registers a user).
  - After `bazis_front update` from 0.3, a conflict of `frontend/src/app/router.tsx` is
    resolved with the file of the template, its pristine copy in `frontend/.bazis/base/`,
    and the changes of the product applied to it again (S048); the screens of the guests
    routed by hand before list the anonymous role in their `roles` instead.
  - The task of a new product (`create_project`, the prompt `new_project`, `bazis new`) and
    that of bazis-front added to a backend: the specs list the anonymous role in `roles` of
    each screen of the guests.
- **A new product requires bazis-front 0.4.0** (it was 0.3.0).

## Changes

- **The catalog is bazis-front 0.4.0** (it was 0.3.1) and **bazis-statusy 2.13.0** (it was
  2.12.0): `package_guide`, `guide_search` and `front_catalog` describe the public screens
  (the guide of 0.3 had a recipe of routes written by hand, which is gone), the contract of
  format 7 (`public` of the custom actions), the custom actions of the toolbar of a list in
  the scenarios, the checks P035, S044–S048 and V020, and the meta `for_transit` of a list
  of bazis-statusy (an anonymous user gets no transit of a route set whose `action_transit`
  is not public). The other packages of the catalog are unchanged, bazis-cli is not in it.
- **The catalog is bazis 2.17.0** (it was 2.16.0): its guide describes the totals of a list
  for dashboards, `meta=aggregates` (sum, avg, min, max, count, grouped by a field or a date
  bucket).
