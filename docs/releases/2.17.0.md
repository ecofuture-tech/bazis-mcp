# bazis-mcp 2.17.0

## Changes

- **The demo database after the end-to-end tests.** The exception of the ban «never drop or
  recreate the database of `.env`» now names the database by what it is, the same words as
  the guide of bazis-front: the database of `.env` created in this session for the new
  product (by `create_project`, or by the agent when it was missing) that holds nothing but
  its demo data. It is recreated once after the last end-to-end tests (then `migrate` and
  `e2e_data`); a database of a product that exists, or a shared one, is never emptied.
- **The guides in one answer.** The rules and the task of a new product read the
  introductions of the guides of the packages chosen, and the sections that one step names,
  in one answer of parallel calls of `package_guide` (the trials of bazis-front 0.13 spent
  17 to 22 answers of the model on the guides).
- **The top bar.** The rules and the task no longer say how many links the top bar shows
  (they said five, the guide about 8 at 1280 px): "The top bar" of the section "The design"
  of the guide says it, and how the top bar folds its links into `nav:more`.
- **After `bazis_front update`.** `npm install` when the update changed `package.json`, then
  `front_status` and the subcommands it names (instead of a fixed list of them); what a new
  version changes in the code of a product is in "Updating the copies" of the guide, which
  the rules no longer retell version by version (0.3 to 0.10).
- **An average** is rounded to the decimal places of its field (it «showed» them: bazis-front
  0.14 drops the trailing zeros of an average).
- **Shorter rules, the same claims.** The rules no longer repeat what the guide of
  bazis-front 0.14.0 says: the limit of the groups of the aggregates (`ERR_AGGREGATE`),
  the time zone of the axis of a chart, the history of the updates, the rename of a
  `photo.png` of the product before the update to 0.10, what to do about an older
  bazis-authing (C005); the step of the screenshots of the task refers to the rules
  instead of repeating them, and the final checks no longer repeat the order of the
  end-to-end tests. The rules of safety (the PID of the backend, the databases, `.env` and
  the secrets) and the names of the final checks are unchanged.
- **The room of the result.** The result of `create_project` of a product with a frontend
  is 33,896 bytes with a project directory of 100 bytes and a database that cannot be
  reached (it was 35,952): its test, which measures it with that directory and the message
  of that database on Linux, whatever the machine, keeps it under 34,000, 2,000 bytes
  below the tenth of the limit of the clients it kept before. A test checks that the
  sections of the guide of bazis-front that the rules and the tasks name exist in the
  guide of the catalog.

## Requirements

- Unchanged: a new project with a frontend requires `bazis-front>=0.13.0` and
  `bazis>=2.25.0`.

## Upgrading

- The projects already created are not rewritten; `use_project` gives them the new rules.
