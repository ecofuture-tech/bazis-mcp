# bazis-mcp 2.9.0

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **The forms of the custom actions** of bazis-front 0.6.0. The rules of the frontend
  (`bazis://rules/frontend`) say how an agent builds them:
  - A page that only sends a custom action (a sign-up, a request) is a spec, `primitive:
    form` with `form.action`, never a custom screen. The sign-up or the request of a guest
    is such a form on a public screen: the properties of the body are named as the fields of
    the entity (a relationship is then a picker, whose items the anonymous role must view,
    S052; a title is translated), the others are labelled with `labels`, and what the action
    returns is shown with `result` (`{title, message}`, whose `{name}` are the properties
    of its `response_model`, S053).
  - The throttle of a public action leaves room for the runs of its end-to-end test, which
    calls it every time (`20/hour` answers 429 after a few runs), and the summary says that
    the throttle is the only guard of a public form against spam.
  - A field of the payload of a transit or of the body of an action has a type that a form
    asks for: an amount is a `Decimal`, typed with a point or the comma of the language;
    another union, an object or another array is typed as JSON (S054). In an extension or a
    custom screen a decimal is the `FieldInput` of its field (its `DecimalInput`), never an
    input or a parse of its own; an average of a tile shows the decimal places of its field
    (`places` of the contract).
  - The top bar moves the links it has no room for into its menu More (`nav:more`) by
    itself, and a tile of a sum takes the whole row of a phone: no links dropped and no
    extension for them. No screen is named `more`, the mark of that menu (S055). The link
    of the form of an action requires its action by itself: it takes no `requires` (S030).
- **The Google login of bazis-authing 2.8**:
  - The rules of every project (`bazis://rules`): the Google login of a browser is `POST
    /google-auth-init/` with the store token as the bearer token, then a window of the same
    browser at the `url` of its answer, never a URL with the store token; its one-time ticket
    is bound to the browser by a SameSite=Lax cookie, so the page is same-site with the API.
  - The rules of the frontend: the login screen of bazis-front 0.7.0 offers Google only with
    bazis-authing 2.8 or newer; `check` warns of the `GET` of an older one (C005), which is
    upgraded (it requires bazis 2.19) before the contract is exported again. A login screen
    of the product opens Google with `loginInWindow` of `frontend/src/app/session.ts`.
- **The history of the transits of bazis-statusy 2.15** (`bazis://rules`): the payload type
  of a transit is the classmethod `Model.get_transit_payload_type(transit)`, the one to
  override; the validated payload is kept in the history record and read with the
  permission `history` of the item (`GET /{item_id}/status_history/`), granted only to the
  roles that may read the payloads; a secret field of a payload model is marked
  `NOT_IN_HISTORY`; the calculated field `status_history` stays the compact history under
  `view`.
- **The update to bazis-front 0.7.0** (`bazis://rules/frontend`): the contract is exported
  again (format 10, C002 until then), a custom screen that only sent a custom action becomes
  the form of the action, an extension that only widened a tile of a sum is removed, a screen
  with the id `more` is renamed (S055), and code that read `CAPABILITIES.authing.token_param`
  uses `loginInWindow`.

## Changes

- **The catalog** describes the latest releases: bazis 2.19.1 (it was 2.17.0: a field with
  `choices` accepts only its choices, a relation by a foreign key with `to_field` is
  identified by the primary key, a computed `SchemaField(source=...)`), bazis-front 0.7.0
  (it was 0.5.0: the forms of the custom actions, the top bar, the decimals, the Google login
  of bazis-authing 2.8, the checks C005 and S052-S055, the contract of format 10),
  bazis-authing 2.8.0 (it was 2.7.0: the `POST` init of Google), bazis-statusy 2.15.0 (it
  was 2.14.0: `status_history`, `NOT_IN_HISTORY`, `get_transit_payload_type`), bazis-bg
  2.7.0 (it was 2.6.0: `task_accepted`, the error of a task shown to its author as `Error`)
  and bazis-users 2.10.1 (it was 2.9.0: the language of a user only among the choices). The
  other packages of the catalog are unchanged; `front_catalog` lists the assets of
  bazis-front 0.7.0.

## Requirements

- **A new project requires `bazis>=2.19.1`** (it was 2.17.0), the release of the catalog,
  which bazis-authing 2.8.0 and bazis-bg 2.7.0 require; **a new product requires
  `bazis-front>=0.7.0`** (it was 0.5.0).
- The extra `test` of bazis-mcp requires `bazis>=2.19.1`.

## Upgrading

- The projects already created keep their requirements: nothing changes until they are
  upgraded. A product whose frontend is upgraded to bazis-front 0.7.0 follows the rules of
  the frontend: `bazis_front update`, then `contract` (format 10), `check`, `screens`, `e2e`
  and `shots`; with bazis-authing and its Google login, bazis-authing 2.8.0 (with
  bazis 2.19) first, else `check` warns C005 and the login screen offers no Google.
- A product with a public form tested end to end gives the throttle of its action room for
  the runs of the tests.
