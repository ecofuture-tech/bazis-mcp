## Features

- **The forms of the custom actions** of bazis-front 0.6.0. The rules of the frontend
  (`bazis://rules/frontend`) say how an agent builds them:
  - A page that only sends a custom action (a sign-up, a request) is a spec, `primitive:
    form` with `form.action`, never a custom screen. The sign-up or the request of a guest
    is such a form on a public screen: the properties of the body are named as the fields of
    the entity (a relationship is then a picker, whose items the anonymous role must view,
    S052; a title is translated), the others are labelled with `labels`, and what the action
    returns is shown with `result` (`{title, message}`, whose `{name}` are the properties
    of its `response_model`, S053).
  - The throttle of a public action leaves room for the runs of its end-to-end test, which
    calls it every time (`20/hour` answers 429 after a few runs), and the summary says that
    the throttle is the only guard of a public form against spam.
  - A field of the payload of a transit or of the body of an action has a type that a form
    asks for: an amount is a `Decimal`, typed with a point or the comma of the language;
    another union, an object or another array is typed as JSON (S054).
  - The top bar moves the links it has no room for into its menu More by itself, and a tile
    of a sum takes the whole row of a phone: no links dropped and no extension for them. The
    link of the form of an action requires its action by itself: it takes no `requires`
    (S030).
- **The update to bazis-front 0.6.0**: the contract is exported again (format 9, C002 until
  then), a custom screen that only sent a custom action becomes the form of the action, and
  an extension that only widened a tile of a sum is removed.
- **A new product requires bazis-front 0.6.0** (it was 0.5.0); the projects already
  created are not affected.
