# bazis-mcp 2.8.0

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **The dashboards** of bazis-front 0.5.0, whose tiles and charts read the aggregates of
  the core (`meta=aggregates` of bazis 2.17.0). The rules of the frontend
  (`bazis://rules/frontend`) say how an agent builds them:
  - A sum, an average, a minimum or a maximum is the `value` of a tile (`sum`, `avg`,
    `min`, `max`) or of a chart (`sum`, `avg`). Items are never counted or summed in the
    browser: what a tile or a chart cannot say (a sum by a category or a related item) is
    `useList` with `meta: ['aggregates']`, `aggregate` and `group` in an extension.
  - A sum or an average of an amount or a quantity with a fraction is of a `DecimalField`
    (the format `decimal` of the contract, S049), and the route set of the entity filters
    by the field (S050).
  - The core answers 400 `ERR_AGGREGATE` over more groups than
    `BAZIS_API_AGGREGATE_GROUPS_MAX` (1000 by default): a chart by day or by week groups
    the days of its periods, so a long one is by month or has a smaller `last`. The axis
    of a chart is in the time zone of the backend, not of the browser.
- **The update to bazis-front 0.5.0**: the contract is exported again (format 8, C002 until
  then), the capability `statusy` needs bazis-statusy 2.13.0 (C004, `front.W006`), and the
  removed `useTransitsOf` and message `board.no_moves` are not used (the transits of a
  list are its `meta: ['for_transit']`, those of an item `useTransits`).
- **A new product requires bazis-front 0.5.0** (it was 0.4.0); the projects already
  created are not affected.

## Changes

- **The catalog is bazis-front 0.5.0** (it was 0.4.0): `package_guide` and `guide_search`
  describe the aggregates of the tiles and the charts, the contract of format 8 (the
  format `decimal`), the moves of a board from `for_transit` of its lists, and the checks
  S049 and S050; `front_catalog` lists the assets of 0.5.0. The other packages of the
  catalog are unchanged, bazis-cli is not in it.
- **The catalog has bazis-statusy 2.14.0** (it was 2.13.0): `for_transit` of a list lists
  the transits of the top level of `state_actions`; the child transits of a
  `TransitRelation` are left out, `state_actions` shows them only nested under their
  parent.
