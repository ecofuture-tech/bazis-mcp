# bazis-mcp 2.6.0

Draft: released after bazis-front 0.3.0, with the catalog rebuilt from its release.

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **The generated screens** (`bazis_front screens` of bazis-front 0.3.0): the agent
  describes a screen in `spec/screens/<id>.yaml` and generates it, never writes its TSX.
  - The task of a product: the spec of a screen, `front_check`, then `bazis_front screens`
    (and `npm install` when it set the dependencies of the components it copied); an
    extension (`extend`) or a custom screen (`custom`) only for what a spec cannot say. A
    change of a product generates its screens again.
  - The rules of the frontend: never edit `frontend/src/screens/generated/` (`front.W010`),
    the texts of the specs in the languages of the product, `bazis_front screens --check`
    in the final checks.
  - `run_command("manage", ["bazis_front", "screens"])` runs it, also with `--check`; never
    with `--force`, which drops the edits of the generated screens: that one is the user's.
    `write_file` never writes `frontend/src/screens/generated/` (a `generated/` directory).
  - `front_status` groups the stale generated screens under `screens`.
- **The screenshots of the screens** (`bazis_front shots` of bazis-front 0.2.0): every
  screen of the specs shot as a role that uses it, in each color mode of the theme, at the
  width of a desktop and of a phone, into `frontend/.scratch/shots/` with a `report.json`
  of what each screen renders wrong (a page that scrolls sideways on a phone, a raw name or
  a UUID shown, a text without contrast, a screen not reached).
  - `run_command("manage", ["bazis_front", "shots"])` runs them (also with `--check`,
    which only compares the generated test with the specs, and `--no-run`).
  - The task of a new product (`new_project`, `bazis new`, `add_package("bazis-front")`)
    has the step of the guide that takes them: with the backend running, `shots`, then the
    errors of the report and what the screenshots show fixed, and the screenshots taken
    again until the report has no error, at most three rounds. It replaces the one look
    at the screens of before.
  - The rules of the frontend (`bazis://rules/frontend`): the screens are done when the
    report has no error and the screenshots pass the checklist of "The visual check" of
    the guide; the final checks have `bazis_front shots --check`, and `bazis_front shots`
    after `npm run e2e`; `frontend/.scratch/` is never committed.
  - `front_screenshot(file, part=1)` gives a screenshot as an image to a client without a
    shell: only a PNG of `frontend/.scratch/shots/`, up to 3,000,000 bytes and 40 images of
    a run; one taller than 1568 px in parts of that height (`part i of n`), which a client
    does not scale down unreadable.
  - The prompt `build_frontend` ends with the screenshots, and `front_status` groups their
    warning `front.W008` (a stale `shots.ts`) under `shots`; the errors of the report, or no
    report (the screenshots not taken), `front.W009`, are its new `shot_errors`: not stale,
    and the work is not done while one of them is not listed to the user after the three
    rounds. `run_doctor` leaves `front.W009` to the tasks of the frontend: one that changes
    no screen, spec or design does not take the screenshots.
  - `front_status` has `reviewed`: the number of the `screens` of the report of the last
    screenshots, of those `looked` at with `front_screenshot` in its run (a screenshot of
    each given whole, every part of a tall one), and the screens `not_looked`; null
    without a report. The rules of the frontend: every screen of the last report is looked
    at, and without a shell the work is not done while `reviewed` names a screen
    `not_looked` that Left for you does not list; each warning of the report is fixed, or
    listed in Left for you with its screen, its code and why it stays (the task of a new
    product and the prompt `build_frontend` say both).

## Changes

- Without a project, `list_packages`, `package_guide` and `guide_search` describe the
  released packages of the catalog, none installed (they answered "No project"), and the
  error "No project" of the other tools names the projects one level below the current
  directory of the server as calls of `use_project`: a client that starts bazis-mcp again
  for the next change of a conversation finds the project it created there.
- A next change asked in the conversation ends with the whole round of the final checks,
  also the checks of the parts it did not change (the rules), and the task of a change of a
  product names them: `run_doctor`, the tests, `front_check`, `front_status`, in
  `frontend/` `tsc`, `lint`, `test`, `build`, `screens --check` and `shots --check`, then
  `npm run e2e` and `bazis_front shots` against the running backend.
- `run_command("npm", ["run", "e2e"])` is `npm run e2e`, as `["e2e"]`; the refusal of
  another script says the form of the arguments.
- The rules of a product: its navigation in groups once it has more than five links, the
  dates of `e2e_data` relative to the day of the run (in UTC, as `expectDay` counts), and a package no longer used left to
  the user to uninstall (nothing of bazis-mcp uninstalls).
- `front_catalog` no longer has `init` for each asset: bazis-front 0.2.0 has no such field
  in its registry, `init` copies the vendored assets, the template and the assets that the
  template `requires` (its entry in `front_catalog`).
- A new product requires bazis-front 0.3.0 (`bazis-front>=0.3.0`), and its `AGENTS.md`
  says where the screenshots go and that the screens are generated from their specs.
- The step of the design in the task of a product names the layers of the design of
  bazis-front 0.2.0: the palette, the fonts, the shape and the surface.
