# bazis-mcp 2.13.0

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **`front_check` notices a stale contract.** When the specs were checked against a
  `contract/contract.json` that `bazis_front status` reports stale (a field, a route or a
  role changed since the last `bazis_front contract`), the result starts with `notice`: the
  issues may come from the old contract (such as P012 and S032 of a field just added), export
  it with `bazis_front contract` from the migrated database and check again. The issues are
  reported all the same, and the tool never exports the contract itself (it depends on the
  migrations and the database). No notice when the specs were not checked against a contract
  (`contract` false, such as before the backend), when only the files generated from it for
  the frontend are stale (`front_status` names them), or when the status cannot be read (a
  route that fails to import): the specs are checked as before.
- **`run_command` and `add_packages` keep the end of an installation.** When `pip install`,
  `npm install`, `npm ci` or `npx playwright install` succeeds, `stdout` and `stderr` are
  their last 20 lines (the line of each package filled the context of the agent); a failure
  keeps the whole end of its output, as every other command does.
- **The rules of every project** (`bazis://rules`):
  - only what the task asks is built (no admin site, extra roles or read-only flags,
    notifications or exports it does not name); what the agent would add goes to Left for
    you;
  - the ASGI app of Bazis serves no Django admin (only `manage.py runserver` does): the agent
    never tests it or counts on it; the users come from `e2e_data`, `createsuperuser` and
    the users route.
- **The rules of the frontend** (`bazis://rules/frontend`): a warning of `bazis_front shots`
  on a component of bazis-front (such as V009 of the name of a file before bazis-front
  0.10.1) is its defect, listed in Left for you and never fixed in the copies.

## Changes

- **The catalog** describes the latest releases: bazis 2.23.0 (it was 2.22.0: the search
  reaches the fields that the card of a route shows), bazis-permit 2.12.0 (it was 2.11.0: the
  same search through the field permissions, it requires bazis 2.23) and bazis-front 0.10.1
  (it was 0.10.0: the template test of the login in any language, the name of a file 44 px
  on a touch screen, the hints of the title of a role, of `requires` of a create form and of
  a tile); the other packages are unchanged.
- The rules and the steps of a product say shorter what they said twice (the sections of
  the guide of bazis-front, the language of the request, the conftest of the skeleton, the
  parts, the checklist and the rounds of the screenshots in step 9) or in more words, so
  that the result of `create_project` for a product, with the new rules, still keeps a tenth
  of the limit of the clients free.

## Requirements

- **A new project requires `bazis>=2.23.0`** (it was 2.22.0) and, with a frontend,
  **`bazis-front>=0.10.1`** (it was 0.10.0); the extra `test` requires `bazis>=2.23.0`.

## Upgrading

- A client that reads `front_check` by its keys sees one more, `notice`, only with a stale
  contract; `checked`, `ok`, `contract`, `errors`, `warnings` and `issues` are unchanged.
- `front_check` of specs checked against a contract also runs `bazis_front status` (about a
  second more on the sample).
- The projects already created are not rewritten: an older product takes bazis-front 0.10.1
  with `pip install -U bazis-front` and `bazis_front update`, as the rules say.
