# bazis-mcp 2.16.0

## Changes

- **The catalog** describes the latest releases: bazis-front 0.13.0 (it was 0.12.0: the new
  look of a product, the starter layers `palette: frost`, `fonts: golos` and
  `surface: lifted`, which `init` names for a new product of either preset, `workspace` and
  `portal`, instead of `indigo`, `inter` and `elevated`; the components on the tokens of
  their surfaces, their controls and their focus, the fields a bottom line; `layout: focus` and `kind: distribution` of a dashboard,
  `display: avatar` and `display: chips` of a field, `header: {search, count}` of a list,
  `count` of a link of the navigation, the plate of the user and the rail of the sidebar; the
  checks S067–S070; the format of the contract stays 12; the choices that the trials missed:
  `mode: both` unless the user asks for one mode, a larger text by `preset: portal`, a top
  bar of about 8 links, a transit for an action by the state of an item); the other packages
  are unchanged.
- **The rules of the frontend** (`bazis://rules/frontend`) send the look of the screens to the
  section "The design" of the guide of bazis-front, its tokens, never literal colors (a
  sentence one character shorter: the result of `create_project` keeps its room).
- **The demo database after the end-to-end tests.** The scenarios left their records in the
  database the user logs in to (`e2e_data` only adds). The ban «never drop or recreate the
  database of `.env`» now has its one exception: the database that `create_project` made in
  the session is recreated once after the last end-to-end tests (then `migrate` and
  `e2e_data`); the rule of `e2e_data` refers to it, and «never remove them, also at the end of
  a phase» is gone. The database of a product that exists, or a shared one, is never emptied:
  `e2e_data` only, as before.
- **Stopping the backend.** The rules said «then stop the backend» without how: now by its
  PID (`kill <PID>`), never `pkill`, `killall` or a pattern, which end other processes of
  the user.
- **A stale claim removed.** The rules said that a tile of a `value` takes the whole row of
  a phone; since bazis-front 0.13 only the last of an odd number of tiles does: removed.

## Requirements

- A new project with a frontend requires **`bazis-front>=0.13.0`** (it was 0.12.0); it still
  requires `bazis>=2.25.0`.

## Upgrading

- The projects already created are not rewritten. An older product takes bazis-front 0.13
  with `pip install -U bazis-front`, `bazis_front update`, `contract` and `design`, as the rules
  say; its look changes as the release notes of bazis-front 0.13.0 say, and the new look of a
  new product is opt-in for it (`palette: frost`, `fonts: golos`, `surface: lifted` in
  `theme.yaml`).
