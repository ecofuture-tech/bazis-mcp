# bazis-mcp 2.4.2

## Changes

- The catalog describes the latest releases: bazis 2.4.2, bazis-permit 2.4.1 and
  bazis-statusy 2.4.1 (the fixes found by the evals of bazis-cli: a project with
  bazis-statusy loads without its database and can be introspected, and the translated
  fields of permit and statusy no longer depend on the languages of the project).
- The `main` branch has the changes of 2.4.1 again: they were released from the tag but
  missing on `main`.
