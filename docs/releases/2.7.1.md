# bazis-mcp 2.7.1

How each tool works and what it refuses is in the guide,
[bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md](../../bazis/contrib/mcp/AGENTS.md).

## Features

- **The dashboards** of bazis-front 0.5.0, whose tiles and charts read the aggregates of
  the core (`meta=aggregates` of bazis 2.17.0). The rules of the frontend
  (`bazis://rules/frontend`) say how an agent builds them:
  - A sum, an average, a minimum or a maximum is the `value` of a tile (`sum`, `avg`,
    `min`, `max`) or of a chart (`sum`, `avg`), never computed in an extension, a hook or a
    request of the product.
  - A sum or an average of an amount or a quantity with a fraction is of a `DecimalField`
    (the format `decimal` of the contract, S049), and the route set of the entity filters
    by the field (S050).
  - The core groups a chart into at most 1000 groups (400 `ERR_AGGREGATE` beyond), and the
    axis of a chart is in the time zone of the backend, not of the browser.
- **The update to bazis-front 0.5.0**: the contract is exported again (format 8, C002 until
  then), and the removed `useTransitsOf` and message `board.no_moves` are not used (the
  transits of a list are its `meta: ['for_transit']`, those of an item `useTransits`).
- **A new product requires bazis-front 0.5.0** (it was 0.4.0); the projects already
  created are not affected.

## Changes

- **The catalog is bazis-front 0.5.0** (it was 0.4.0): `package_guide`, `guide_search` and
  `front_catalog` describe the aggregates of the tiles and the charts, the contract of
  format 8 (the format `decimal`), the moves of a board from `for_transit` of its lists,
  and the checks S049 and S050. The other packages of the catalog are unchanged, bazis-cli
  is not in it.
